Welcome to George's Slop Garden

Hi, I'm George. I build software for a living. I'm a husband and a dad.

I made this site to host whatever I feel like sharing.

I have limited free time; don't we all. I'm too impatient to build everything from scratch, so I'm leaning into agentic development. My personal projects aren't production systems that users rely on. I don't care that there's slop under the hood that I didn't write or even review.

I'm also cheap. For this site, I use free tier LLMs and infrastructure. That lets me avoid unnecessary stress about subscription fees and runaway costs. When I hit free tier limits, I'm reminded to take a break and do something else. It works, and it didn't cost me anything but time; I'm satisfied.

Contact me at prophetzopu@gmail.com if you want. I don't check the inbox frequently, so sorry in advance if it takes me a while to respond.
