Why Slop Garden?

June 19, 2026
meta site

I made this site because I wanted a place to put stuff. Simple as that.

I build software for a living. I’m a husband and a dad. Free time is scarce, and when inspiration strikes, I need to move fast before it fades. I got tired of letting ideas die because setting up yet another blog or project page felt like work.

So I built Slop Garden — a single-stream dumping ground for whatever I feel like sharing. Code, notes, half-baked projects, tutorials, random thoughts. It all goes into one chronological log, sorted by date, no buckets to agonize over.

The Philosophy

I’m not trying to build a brand or grow an audience. This is a pressure-release valve. Here are the principles I’m running on:

Riding the Inspiration Wave — Work on things when the urge hits, drop them when it’s gone. I use AI to blast through the tedious boilerplate so I don’t burn through my limited attention span before getting to the fun part. Once the initial burst of energy runs out, the project or post is done — unless I get inspired to revisit it later.

Aggressive Frugality — Keep the bill at zero. I treat free enterprise tiers like a public utility. If a hobby project needs a recurring subscription, I find a free alternative or walk away. Free tier limits remind me to take a break and do something else. It works, and it didn’t cost me anything but time.

Take What the AI Gives You — I don’t waste time over-engineering the site setup or rewriting what the AI spits out. I just ship it. If the code looks bloated under the hood, who cares? It works. The site you’re reading right now was scaffolded in minutes using free AI tooling.

How It Works

The entire site is one page at /log with a simple search bar. Each entry has a title, date, summary, and some tags. That’s it. No database, no server — just static files generated by Astro, hosted somewhere free. That’s the whole site. No newsletters, no comments, no analytics. Just a place to throw things into the dirt before the inspiration fades.
